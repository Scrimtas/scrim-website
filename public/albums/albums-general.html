Albums superhell

So, here we are. Obviously so very subjective and only rated based on vibed with-didn't vibe with kinda scale. Also, the genre system is suuuuper simplified, just for tags convinience and my own sanity. I'd try to name the genres more specifically on each respective page when (if) I get to making them, haha

  • 5 stars = personal favorite among my top albums
  • 4 stars = definitely a good one, I like it a lot!
  • 3 stars = I consider the album to be good or like at least okay, but didn't vibe with it as much, often the reason might be I listened it in a wrong mood
  • 2 stars = really didn't vibe with it, not my thing basically that is hard for me to, well, enjoy or appreciate
  • 1 star = supposed to mark the albums I sincerely hated but it's hard for me to recall any album like this, to be honest??
