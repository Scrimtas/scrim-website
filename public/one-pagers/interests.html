Beloveds

So, you've entered the yapping zone. Or, more likely, a simple cheat sheet for myself to keep a track of various stuff I'm interested in + all the media I watched/played/read/etc and loved. Yep, no hatewatches included. I might not consider absolutely every thing mentioned necessary 'good' media, but I can say that they all are dear to me. You can take this as my 'recomendations' list of you feel like it (as well as I'm very very open to recs as well if you have something on you mind that you think I might like), but there are controversial and disturbing media at the list, and you know what you are okay or not okay with better than I do, dear stranger from the internet. A wip, just like everything else here, especially the film list...

  • Stands for media I'm currently watching/reading/etc! Maybe not, well, Right Now, but they're overall wip and are in my focus right now!
  • Stands for things that changed something in my brain's chemistry and I'm very likely to never really be 'over' them. My extremely biased favorites.
  • Stands basically all other media I like a lot!
  • Stands for media I liked but didn't finish yet, or in games case it might been about the situation where I have been mostly watching over my friend's sholder and definitely missed quite a lot of things that I couldn't have overlook if I was playing it firsthands. Maybe I don't plan to finish some of these things even [e.g. warrior cats or All sherlock holmes books ever created, y'know], but usually I do, just...Sometime in future. I really hope to. I'm totally fine with spoilers for not finished things, by the way (they don't ruin my experience and im very likely to forget them anyway :р)
