About me!

So, a place for me to tell a little bit about myself, eh? It's always a difficult thing. People are a complecated subject to describe, especially considering sad yet expectable perspective distortion when the subject is, well, you personally. What do I know about myself? I am friendly and hardworking. I am painfully perfectionistic and self-sabotaging nervous wreck, but I'm working on implementing 'let it exist first' and 'the good is the enemy of the perfect' ideas in my head. I am very introspective and prone to intellectualising, I think #a lot. I am less edgy then I used to think of myself. I'm easily irritated, but I learnt to manage that, I hate conflicts and I'm generally quite easygoing and chill unless in super stressful environment. I am very curious, both for better and for worse (i'm nosy and I WANT TO LEARN ABOUT EVERYTHING even if that's impossible and I'm trapped in dreadful limits of a lifetime). I am many diffirent things and, just like anything in this world, I'm instantly changing.

I'm a 25 y.o. self-diagnosed girlfailure from Russia with Belarusian-Polish ancestry. Languages-wise, I have quite a decent English (C1-leaning B2 I assume, I never checked properly) and A2-kinda Czech and probably even worser French which I loved learning but have been neglecting recently ( Un jour je serai de retour près de toi, watch out). I'm a hobbyist artist generally since forever, but I've only been drawing in digital regularly something like 3 or 4 years ago. I'm madly in love with storytelling and have a huge interest for original stories and original characters (in a broad sense, I love interesting fan characters as well), and I have a plenty myself! :]

My fascination with oc culture brought me to toyhouse back in 2019, and toyhouse introduced me to html, in-line css and bootstrap, to some extent. Not that I wrote anything myself, but I learnt quite a lot from editing templates written by other people and frankensteining various snippets like crazy. In 2025 I wanted to share my artfight templates (back then AF was using bbcode specifically) and I've faced the problem of 'augh how do I make a preview for toyhouse users in any other way but using screenshots' and, well. I remade then with html (and in-line css), which was my very first attempt to make an html/css page completely myself. I've made some other toyhouse-specific stuff for personal usage later which I shared with others as a free template. And then my friend gifted me a toyhouse premium which provided me access to global css earlier this year, and I wasn't a normal person ever since. Good thing: I understood a lot of stuff. Bad thing: I'm insane now and here's the direct consequence in front of y'all. I have so many things to learn, but I do enjoy the journey!

I'm fond of visual arts, XX century history, cinema, animation (especially 2D ohhhh), videogames and a plenty of musical genres. Books also, but I'm still struggling with texts-related burnt out after my bachelor thesis (I did it to myself). Unfortunatyle, my knowledges on all the subjects mentioned are very humble. Good thing: I have so many beautiful things yet to discover! I track my top favorites (and something I currently watch/read/play) here, feel free to have a look!

FAQ

- Who's Scrim?

- Well, that's me! To be more specific, Scrimtas (as well as dirived from it Scrim and Tas) is both my online alias and a name of my mascot (that silly fluffy border collie dog you might see here and there). The nickname has a certain (and very random) etymology, but no attached meaning otherwise.

- Your mascot is furry and I saw your furry drawings. Are you a furry?

- Short answer? You are free to believe in whatever if it makes you sleep better at night. More elaborate answer: no, not really, but it doesn't offend me anyhow if people assume that I am! I started learning how to draw with animal characters, kitties mostly (warrior cats girl detected), and when I wantd to express more 'complecated' and close to my life scenarios I needed something more humanoid. But learning a whole new thing was scary, so creatures with overall human anatomy except for heads and some other minor extra traits like tails were a very handy decision. Plus, lots of artists I admired of highschool had fursonas or generally anthro/furry characters and I found it cool and neat, and of course I wanted to have characters like that too! Even when I became more or less confident in depicting humans, most of my characters still had (and still have) alternative anthro animals designs, just because it's neat and makes me smile :з

So, yeah, I mostly use a term 'anthro' to refer to such characters myself and don't have any subcultural identification with furries as people, but I won't correct you if you use furry term for say my ocs or drawings because I genuinely can't care less and this discourse is endlessly stupid, in my opinion. And of course I still follow quite a lot of furry artists, I think they're rad and I have nothing but a sheer respect for people who can stylize creatures, basically nonexistent in real world, and balance humanoid and animalistic traits.

- What's the point of this site?

- In a grand scheme of things, it does nothing and just exists. Nothing in life matters (/positive)! To me personally? It's my cherished half-personal-half-public diary and a place to both yap about my interests and collect the things I love in one place And a great opportunity to tinker with html and css. Aaaand additionally making a personal website was sort of a dream for me for quite a long time, I've been staring to all the cool kids indie web pages for years, but lacked both knowledges and courage. Still lack of both, objectively, but I'm slowly getting better! 😎

Art-wise? Really need to finish my art trade part and an long-started gift for my bestfriend. Otherwise I'm very original characters-oriented, and I need to finally design so many story-significant blorbos from my mind ough.

Irl? I need to do many things, objectively, but my obvious priority rn is to become tragically employed ✊
