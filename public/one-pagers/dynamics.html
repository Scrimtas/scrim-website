Casey Hartley & Mae Borowski and Gregg Lee

Night in the Woods

It's sort of a test entry, so. Messy ramblings ahead, I believe?

I loved the experience of my first playthrough dearly, but, unironically, my favorite part of the story narrative was tied to a guy who never appeared in the plot. Well, never appeared in the plot outside of missing posters, like 10 vague dialogue lines about him best and eventually a confirmed information of his short life's abrupt and unfair end. As a player you never ever see Casey, you see memories of him instead, and a massive void his absence has left in his school friend group. Or maybe not a void really, but, hmm, a ghost? A missing friend, a missing band drummer, a missing guy you all used to know.

As I've already mentioned, Very Few things are actually known about Casey Hartley. An only child probably, from another working class family, very likely. Most of Possum Springs' kids have quite a similar fate, you live in a bumfuck where nothing ever happens, work the same either physical or 'clerk' retail job your parents probably do, and if you're lucky enough you eventually get used to it or maybe even learn to enjoy it, to an extent. A fate he really didn't want for himself. He was an erratic troublemaker and a delinquent, who never wanted anything more than to escape Possum Springs. I LOVE the headcanon that Die Anywhere Else lyrics were by him, both because the motif and the fair amount of #teenage angst and desperation and because, haha. He literally died. Here exactly. Sigh. And, well, Casey was also a guy whose psychological portrait would fit the 'cutting all ties with his past life without even saying goodbye to anyone' scenario, at least just enough to make it possible for his best friend who missed him like hell to believe in it. Which is quite an uncommon characterisation, if you ask me.

I love that it was Casey who was Gregg's number one bestie originally, not Mae. I love that part where Mae described him as a cat overall very similar to her but a bit taller and with orange hair (which honestly got me thinking for a while that he was also black cat but with ginger 'bangs'/head fluff), like, I don't know, their external similarity is just so dear to me for some reason. Chaotic sibling coded kitties. Gregg and Mae were best friends to and played in a band together, but, well, Casey used to be also there. So all their in-game hangouts inevitably have Casey haunting the narrative crazy style. And goddd this is so horrible they basically meet their best friend's murders and…Can't do anything.

Sidebar note: The theory that Casey and Eide are the same person is quite interesting for me and I kind of like it, but I don't really believe it might be a canon thing? Considering that the cultists should have sacrificed anything and there were zero missing people after Casey's kidnaping, as far as I know? Plus it looks very ooc considering anything we know about Casey to be 'lawful' and to work with people who he (mutually) despised. He also doesn't strike me as something with impulse control fine enough not to let his friends know that's /him/. Too many details that just don't really make sense to me. Still a neat one nevertheless!
