My characters

Frankly, one of the reasons I wanted to make this website was to backup my characters' lore somewhere outside of toyhouse, just in case. Good thing for me: I'm basically planning to cross-post here their toyhouse info, on the same template + now I have an extra motivation to work on their pages more systematically in my spare time! Bad thing: I have more than a hundred of active characters and many ocs still on the conceptualisation/figuring details out stage, thus I have no idea how many time it will take me to migrate at least my mains here. Also I have no idea how many of my neocities storage space they will bite off lmao (which is solvable and not today-me problem anyway)...In any case, for now this page is still a placeholder, leading to my toyhouse :р

For now the layout mimics my toyhouse directory, very likely to change it in future! The lighter background stands for sort of a crurrent priority.
