Chainsmoker cat (2026)

watched 08 jul 2026 - 18 oct 2026

Ohh. My first log is a haterrr log, because [im hater at heart] it’s always easier for me to say something about stuff I either really dislike, or about stories that are like 3/5, a-okay, have their pros, have their cons, have cool concepts that were never embodied the way they deserved. So, speaking of cool concepts and wasted potentials...

I’ve heard about it from my bro when the first episode was just released, and he was like ‘there's a new anime about cats with very gross depiction of smoke addiction (and possibly addictions in a broad sense but it’s too early to speak about it anyway) and is basically a trainspotting for girls’ and I was SO hooked by this description. Ngl I was disappointed a bit when I realised ‘the cats’ were nekos, and admittedly I’m not a target audience of kemonomimi characters. On the other hand, depicting traditionally very cute character types as someone not-necessary-nice and basically being a marginalized community of their world sounded curious, and I don’t mind stories featuring character types I'm not normally very fond of if they’re written in an interesting way and the author knows what they do.

So I did find the gimmick on its own very intriguing. And the opening (aka the coolest thing about the whole show) basically consisting of various film hommages only convinced me on my first impression that it's gonna be the anime trainspotting retelling kinda story. So I do not conceal the fact that my worst enemy were my expectations, really, but it Did make my overall disapointment so much worse. I like Trainspotting (1996), I love exploration of addiction, dependency and 'best friends available' sort of dinamics, I AM a sucker for underdogs when they're written decently and their creator actually cares about them and doesn't Just treat them and their problems as a (barely funny) joke.

The first episode was, well, okay? It was enough for me to understand that local humour is not my cup of tea (as im not into 'haha gross' jokes and characters embarassing themselves all of the time AND especially hentai-esque joke. just no), but it's not that much of a dealbreaker on its own if there's anything more to it. Yani Neko's nightmare and her sencere fear leading to her (obviously impulsive) decision to get rid of her addiction seemed to be maybe a simple, but still interesting beginning of the story plot, but, well. Haha. There happened to be no plot. And, well, look, I'm very okay with sitcom-esque plot structure, slice of life genre and so on, character dynamics-based stories tend to be the most interesting for me actually. And, still, there're no actual character dinamics in the show, maybeee some hints to what might be character dinamics if there were characters instead of a set of caricatures. Worldbuilding aspect of neko social issues might also be such an interesting thing to explore, but, still, nothing really happens on that side either, potentially interesting topics are just vaguely mentioned as a one-episode-joke and don't get any attention later. So I've dropped watching on 9th episode with a tired dead stare, although I did plan to finish the season originally but I've been procrastinating it so hard already lmao I obviously has zero interest/hope by that time.

Okay no actually there was something cool in anime in additon to the aforementioned opening. Yakuneko is my precious mewmew and im stealing rescuing her from this awful place /hj
