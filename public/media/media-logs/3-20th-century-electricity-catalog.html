20th Century Electricity Catalog (2026)

watched 5 oct 2026 - 14 oct 2026

I know the official English name adaption of the title is the Sparks of Tomorrow but nuh-ah I'm not calling it this. Not as bad as Journal with Witch imo, but still. Why did you do this. Both is okayyy and would be fine as a names on their own, but I think the original ones fit just so much better.

Might not write as much here, as I've watched the show some time ago, and some of the thoughts and impressions I had might have faded away, but it was a lot of fun! Part of the fan for me was sponsored with my engineer brother's live 'well yes but actually no' live reaction on Kihachi's inventions :D

I love XX century and settings that are strongly inspired with it are typically the ones I like a lot! And I do love mechanic/engineer/inventor kind of characters, Especially when they actually have a specialization/a specific field of knowledge they're good at and they're not just an universal soldier who's a genius in everything (im definitely not looking at a certain Tangled character right now), so the premise was already something that had me quite invested.

Considering the story is about grief (an oh the narrative ghost was Good) and learning how to live again after the loss of a person who was everything and most of interpersonal conflicts here are the direct consequence of people overthinking stuff instead of actually talking about what's on their mind with other people And that you can't build anything new blatantly copying others' works and thoughts? I'm very much a target audience, as expected. And I really appreciated that the story is both not unapologetically long and Actually finished in one season, I don't think I can stress enough how much respect I have for authors who don't keep their audience in ongoing 300+ episodes torture labyrinth.

The story isn't very complicated and is actually quite naive, but not that that's a bad thing. I think that's a shame there wasn't much anime titles that convey a kind adventurous stories, you know? Although I'm not a diehard anime fan who watches this many titles, so glad to be wrong here, sincerely. Anyway, I got distracted. All characters actually have their quite plain but still making sense and working just fine character arcs, which is cool! The artstyle is quite generic which is well a-okay not much to add to this, But the emotions coding looked quite unique? Reminded me of, mm, One Piece i think, but that's just a random association, not that I'm ever watching it to tell for sure anyway </з

Side note: the anime features...Surprisingly, more queer people than I expected?? Expected, well, none, got...3? 4? Depending on whether Seiroku was bi or not

Another side note: okay, the final episodes dynamics between Kihachi and Yosuke was...Bizarre, to say the least... 🚬
