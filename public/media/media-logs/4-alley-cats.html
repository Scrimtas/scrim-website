Alley Cats (2026)

watched 28 sept. 2026 - 30 sept. 2026

As I try to leave notes on both (somewhat) recently watched stuff and my top favorites, I absolutely have to do it when both things overlap.

Content warnings for death, suggestive/sexual topics and overall some gross stuff is referenced quite commonly in a show.

Well, I know the show is mostly criticized for 'Ricky Gervais doing the same thing he's been doing for years' like repeating himself and exploring the same topics he normally does. The last imo is an odd thing to blame a comic or whomever honestly because well yeah people normally have those specific few themes they care about the most / bugged by the most? But except for that I can't really say much here, just because I'm unfortunately rather uneducated about British comedy scene (something I hope to fix but it's an unnecessary sidebar so whatever), I believe that all of this might be very much true, but I'm personally blissfully unaware 😎

The show itself is, well, adorable, in my opinion. I'm objectively biased, but I'm way too attached to the silly cynical cats to the point of making several fanarts although I'm very unconfident in drawing ordinary animals. When it's adorable or (south-park kind of) dumb, it is adorable or dumb. And when it addresses something heavy (death topics mostly), it Does feel like a gutpunch. I know the show might be described as depressing, I saw it more like a 'shit happens, life goes on' kind of story. And, well, I genuinely loved it how the theme of death (both 'expected' and sudden) was treated. Also enjoyed character design and all of the sudden musical-like elements very much :D

What I didn't enjoy, though, is like a large proportion of Puke-centered jokes that were...About a straight-up rape, frankly? It would feel way better if other animal species in the show had the same level of intelligence as, well, cats, the change that would make Puke still a hypersexual pervert but he would be, well, a xenophile I assume? In this scenario. Because the incidents referenced is show don't feel just idk ew or gross, they feel disturbing as fuck
