  • 5 stars = personal fave (extremely subjective). Not a marker of a good media by definition, rather a marker of my attachment to it.
  • 4 stars = definitely a good story I enjoyed an unironically normal amount :]
  • 3 stars = good or at least okay, decent story I didn't vibe with as much but maybe in other mood it would work better for me!
  • 2 stars = A dissappointment tier. It is Bad but has at least any redeemable qualities in my opinion, or a genuinely compelling concept. A shitty story, but still a story nevertheless.
  • 1 star = THE dissappointment tier. EVIL + genuinely a waste of time I distaste. A rare one honestly, I rarely dislike something I chose to spend time on this much.
