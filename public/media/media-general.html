Media thoughts

Hopefully would add more soon! Not sure about the page template I want to use in future but yeah for now it at least exists ig. Spoilers ahead, I might be more or less detailed on a whim, and I tend to be quite vague here, but chances I can mention something of a spoiler nature are quite high nevertheless.

  • 5 stars = personal fave (extremely subjective). Not a marker of a good media by definition, rather a marker of my attachment to it.
  • 4 stars = definitely a good story I enjoyed an unironically normal amount :]
  • 3 stars = good or at least okay, decent story I didn't vibe with as much but maybe in other mood it would work better for me!
  • 2 stars = A disappointment tier. It is Bad but has at least any redeemable qualities in my opinion, or a genuinely compelling concept. A shitty story, but still a story nevertheless.
  • 1 star = THE disappointment tier. EVIL + genuinely a waste of time I detest. A rare one honestly, I rarely dislike something I chose to spend time on this much.
