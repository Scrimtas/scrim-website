Hi there!

You've discovered my humble and currently a heavy work in progress pet project I use to get more comfortable tinkering with html/css (and js at some point) and generally to collect my fave things in one place. I promise, it would become a neater place with time :D

I believe that'd be a right thing to let you know about the Content warnings? For now I believe this website is rather family friendly and I think I'd prefer to keep its relatively neutral tone, so no sudden jumpscares for sure. I'm planning to mark possibly triggering content appropriately, but please keep in mind that I'm an adult person. Thus, you can see some swearing here and there and there might be depictions of moderate violence/blood, as well as artistic nudity, on my artworks. Furthermore, both my own stories and stories of the media I admire often deal with darker topics, which, as I said, I'll try my best to mark properly when discussing, but still an important head-ups.

Accessibility-wise, I try my best to avoid flashing animated content (and gifs in general, except for site links buttons) and clashing colors, so hopefully no jumpscares on this side. I use javascript for tag system in art gallery/diary entries + to stop the gifs on the homepage, so the sorting features and stop/play gif feature won't work if you have it turned off, but except for that it shouldn't affect your experience much.
